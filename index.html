About

Background

I have worked as an Energy Trading Analyst at Drax Power, supporting the GB wholesale power trading desk with forward price forecasting, renewable-driven revenue modelling, and reporting automation. My focus was on translating market fundamentals, generation mix, interconnector flows, weather-driven renewable output into forecasts that hold up under real trading conditions.

Before moving into energy markets, I spent three years as a Software Developer at Qualcomm India, building embedded software for commercial chipset platforms — a background that shows up now in how I approach modelling: rigorous, reproducible, and built to be automated rather than run by hand.

I hold an MSc in Data Science and Analytics from the University of Leeds (Distinction) and a B.Tech in Electronics & Communication Engineering from NIT Durgapur (First Class Distinction).
